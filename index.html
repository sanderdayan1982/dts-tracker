Formula: Net Treasury Flow = (Withdrawals − Debt Redemptions) − (Deposits − Debt Issues) · Mosler-style.
Sign: 🟢 positive = net fiscal injection — government creates net financial assets (NFA) for the non-government sector · 🔴 negative = net fiscal drain (NFA destruction).
Reserves ≠ NFA: day-to-day bank reserve impact is −ΔTGA (TGA down = reserves added; TGA up = reserves drained). A bond-financed deficit creates NFA while leaving reserves ~flat — issuance drains what spending injects.
Z-score: ±1σ = normal range · |Z| > 2 = extraordinary daily flow (rolling 30-business-day window, always computed on the daily-flow basis).
